# Ace Digital OS

Operate surface. Light by default, dark available. Paper ground, ink text, one navy accent (`hsl(215 84% 18%)` in light, `hsl(210 62% 72%)` in dark). System sans. 44px controls, 8px radius, borders instead of glow.

The working record is [docs/design/system.md](docs/design/system.md). The review that produced it is [docs/design/audit.md](docs/design/audit.md).
